Stock Picks Desk1-month ideas · US & India
Not financial advice. These are research ideas built from public news and price data, not a recommendation for your situation. Short-term trades can lose money quickly. Size positions so a stop-loss hit is survivable, and check live prices with your broker before placing any order.
Every batch of picks is kept here and scored against its own buy/target/stop levels. Scoring is conservative: if the stop and the target are both touched on the same day, the stop counts. Returns are measured from each pick's reference close.

How the picks are chosen

The horizon is 1 month at most, so this isn't long-term value investing. It's a catalyst + relative-strength screen:

  1. Market regime first. Figure out which sectors are rising while the index falls. In late Sep 2026: US energy and healthcare, Indian pharma, metals and defence.
  2. A dated catalyst inside the window. Earnings, a contract award, a broker upgrade, or monthly volume data. Something specific has to happen in the next ~30 days.
  3. Hard numbers behind the story. Estimates, margins, order values and price levels, each linked to its source on the card.
  4. A defined exit before entry. Every pick has a buy zone, a sell target and a stop-loss. The target is set near a recent high or at the size of move the catalyst justifies. The stop sits below nearby support and is usually a smaller distance than the target, so the reward is bigger than the risk.

Reading the levels

  • Today's price: the latest close, refreshed automatically after each market close. Before the first refresh it shows the reference close from the research date.
  • Buy zone: the range to enter in. If price has already run above it, skip it. Chasing ruins the risk/reward.
  • Sell target: where to take profit. Stop-loss: where to exit if you're wrong. Both are closing-basis guides.
  • R:R: reward-to-risk, measured from the middle of the buy zone.

How the data stays fresh

A GitHub Action (.github/workflows/update-prices.yml) runs scripts/update_prices.py after the NSE and NYSE closes every weekday. It pulls daily bars from Yahoo Finance, updates the prices and scores every past pick in the Track record tab. To add a new batch, add an entry at the top of data/picks.js. Older batches automatically become history.

Honest limitations

  • Prices on the research date came from news and quote pages, not an exchange feed. One reference (LLY) is a Sep 28 close.
  • A 1-month window is mostly noise and sentiment. Even a well-reasoned pick is close to a coin flip with better odds and a planned exit.
  • Brokerage, taxes (STT/STCG in India, short-term capital gains in the US) and slippage are not included in the returns shown.
